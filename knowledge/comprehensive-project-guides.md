# Comprehensive Industry Project & Solution Guide

ACEVA Technology designs, engineers, and deploys custom software tailored to specific industry workflows. Below is an illustrative guide to the main solution types: what ACEVA can build and the features such systems usually include. These are capability descriptions, not a record of completed client work — ACEVA's documented client work is only what `client-projects.md` names. Technology choices follow the default stack in the technology guide and are decided per engagement.

---

## 1. Restaurant, Food Service & Cloud Kitchen Systems

### What ACEVA Builds
Custom digital ordering portals, mobile customer apps, table booking engines, kitchen display systems (KDS), and inventory management platforms for restaurants, cafes, cloud kitchens, and franchises.

### Key Features
- **Online Menu & Direct Ordering**: High-resolution food catalog with customization options (addons, spice levels, dietary tags) and instant checkout.
- **Table Reservation & Booking**: Real-time table selection, slot availability, and instant SMS/Email booking confirmations.
- **Kitchen Display System (KDS)**: Tablet-optimized live screen for kitchen staff to receive, track, and update order statuses in real time.
- **POS & Payment Gateway Integration**: Accepts credit cards, mobile wallets, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and cash on delivery with automated sales reporting.
- **Customer Loyalty Program**: Points, discounts, digital coupons, and repeat customer retention analytics.

---

## 2. E-Commerce Platforms & Retail Management

### What ACEVA Builds
Custom e-commerce platforms, multi-vendor marketplaces, inventory control systems, point-of-sale (POS) synchronization, and customer account portals for retail brands and wholesalers.

### Key Features
- **Product Catalog & Search**: Instant multi-attribute filtering (category, size, color, price, availability) and search autocomplete.
- **Cart & Secure Checkout**: High-converting cart with discount codes, guest checkout, tax calculation, and multi-currency support.
- **Inventory & Order Management**: Real-time stock level synchronization across online stores and physical retail locations.
- **Shipment & Delivery Tracking**: Automated tracking links sent to customers via email/SMS with courier integration.
- **Admin Analytics Dashboard**: Sales performance, top-selling items, customer lifetime value, and revenue reports.

---

## 3. Healthcare, Clinics & Telehealth Platforms

### What ACEVA Builds
Patient portals, doctor appointment scheduling platforms, telehealth video consultation systems, electronic medical record (EMR) integrations, and clinic administrative dashboards.

### Key Features
- **Doctor Profiles & Appointment Booking**: Browse doctors by specialty, view availability slots, and book appointments online.
- **Patient Portal**: Secure access to medical history, lab test reports, digital prescriptions, and doctor notes.
- **Telehealth Video Consultations**: Web-based video calling for virtual doctor appointments without installing extra software.
- **Automated Appointment Reminders**: Reduces no-shows via automated SMS and email reminders.
- **Billing & Insurance Tracking**: Patient invoice generation, payment processing, and claim record tracking.

---

## 4. Real Estate, Construction & Property Management

### What ACEVA Builds
Property listing portals, virtual tour showcases, tenant portals, construction job tracking systems, maintenance work order dispatching, and landlord dashboards.

### Key Features
- **Interactive Property Search**: Filter listings by location, price, property type, bedrooms, amenities, and map view.
- **Tenant & Rent Portal**: Online rent payments, digital lease agreement sign-offs, and maintenance request tickets.
- **Job & Site Inspection Tracking**: Construction milestone tracking, worker daily logs, material inventory, and photo reports.
- **Document Repository**: Secure file storage for blueprints, permits, contracts, and inspection certificates.

---

## 5. Logistics, Transport & Supply Chain Platforms

### What ACEVA Builds
Fleet management dashboards, dispatch control towers, driver mobile apps, warehouse management systems (WMS), and shipment tracking portals.

### Key Features
- **Dispatch Control Tower**: Real-time overview of active shipments, driver assignments, route statuses, and delay alerts.
- **Driver Mobile App**: Turn-by-turn route view, job acceptance, customer contact, and electronic proof of delivery (e-signature & photo).
- **Client Tracking Portal**: Public or authenticated shipment tracking link showing live delivery milestones.
- **Warehouse Inventory Management**: Stock receiving, bin placement, picking lists, and barcode scanning integration.

---

## 6. Education, Schools & E-Learning Platforms

### What ACEVA Builds
Learning management systems (LMS), online course portals, school administrative systems, student dashboards, and virtual classroom integrations.

### Key Features
- **Course & Lesson Management**: Video lesson hosting, downloadable resources, assignments, and interactive quizzes.
- **Student & Parent Portal**: View course progress, attendance history, assignment grades, and teacher announcements.
- **Fee Payment & Billing**: Online tuition fee payments with automated receipts and invoice tracking.
- **Certificate Generation**: Automated digital certificate issuance upon course completion.
