# How ACEVA Works

ACEVA uses five phases: Understand, Design, Build, Launch, and Improve. Work is made visible through weekly working demos, written decisions, risks, open questions, and clear ownership.

## Understand

Define the business problem, constraints, success criteria, scope boundaries, and feasibility before estimating. Feasibility is reviewed by a senior engineer.

Discovery considers the business objective, target audience, deliverables, features, integrations, content readiness, existing technology, launch requirements, and constraints.

## Design

Plan real user journeys, including mobile, alongside architecture and written acceptance criteria.

## Build

Provide a working demo every week, written decisions and risks, and access to the client’s repository from day one. AI-accelerated work remains senior-reviewed.

## Launch

Perform mobile and browser QA, security basics, content review, analytics, and deployment under accounts the client controls. Provide a launch checklist and named post-launch bug responsibility.

## Improve

Provide monitoring and human-reaching alerts, a prioritized improvement list after real usage, and documentation another team can use.

## Timeline guidance

Project timelines depend on scope. Do not invent timelines to win a meeting or guarantee a delivery date before scope and senior review.
