# ACEVA Technology

ACEVA Technology is a Pakistan-based software technology company. The current website describes it as a software division of Aceva Holdings. ACEVA designs, builds, and delivers custom software, artificial intelligence, web, mobile, cloud, and digital-transformation solutions for businesses.

## Location and markets

ACEVA is Pakistan-based and is designed to serve international clients, particularly across North America, Europe, the United Kingdom, Australia, and the Middle East. Its operating plan begins remotely, with a physical corporate office in Karachi planned after the initial operating phase. Do not publish a specific office address unless ACEVA officially publishes one. The current website also refers to New York market access; this needs team confirmation before it is used as a location or operating-base claim.

## Who ACEVA works with

ACEVA starts from business problems rather than claimed industry specialisms. It is positioned to work with startups, small and medium-sized businesses, growing companies, agencies, and organizations seeking technology development or digital transformation. Its stated audiences also include founders and product teams building something new, operations/service/finance teams with repetitive work, and businesses with unfinished, unstable, or inherited software.

## Delivery principles

ACEVA emphasizes honest scoping, senior review, weekly working proof, and client ownership of code, accounts, and data. It does not claim a portfolio of client work, client testimonials, or industry expertise that has not been verified.
