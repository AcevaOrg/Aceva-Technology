# ACEVA Technology

ACEVA Technology (written "Aceva Technology" in the website footer; the name is singular, never the plural form) is a Pakistan-based software technology company. The current website describes it as the software division of Aceva Holdings, established to deliver custom software engineering with discipline: honest scoping, senior code review, weekly working proof, and clean ownership handover. ACEVA designs, builds, and delivers custom software, artificial intelligence, web, mobile, cloud, and digital-transformation solutions for businesses.

Leadership and company details:

- No founder, CEO, director or individual team member is named on acevatech.com. The company page lists five senior roles by title only — Technical Lead; Backend & AI; Frontend & Product; DevOps & Security; Product & QA Lead — plus junior engineers under named mentors, and says team photographs and individual biographies will be published as real profiles are confirmed. Pulse must not name or guess any person. If asked who founded or runs ACEVA, say that leadership profiles are not published yet and point to /contact.
- No legal entity name, company registration number, founding date or head-office street address is published. The relationship to Aceva Holdings is stated on the website but is not documented further.
- The only published contact details are the ones in `contact.md`: email, phone, WhatsApp and the four social profiles.

## Location and markets

ACEVA is Pakistan-based and is designed to serve international clients, particularly across North America, Europe, the United Kingdom, Australia, and the Middle East. The website says ACEVA sells from New York "because that is where we get proximity to buyers, but delivery is not tied to a location — we run engagements with clients wherever they are", and summarises this as "New York insight. Global execution." The published phone number is a Pakistan (+92) number. Its operating plan begins remotely, with a physical corporate office in Karachi planned after the initial operating phase. Do not publish a specific office address: none is published.

## Who ACEVA works with

ACEVA starts from business problems rather than claimed industry specialisms. It is positioned to work with startups, small and medium-sized businesses, growing companies, agencies, and organizations seeking technology development or digital transformation. Its stated audiences also include founders and product teams building something new, operations/service/finance teams with repetitive work, and businesses with unfinished, unstable, or inherited software.

## Delivery principles

ACEVA emphasizes honest scoping, senior review, weekly working proof, and client ownership of code, accounts, and data. It does not claim a portfolio of client work, client testimonials, or industry expertise that has not been verified. The website says it started from zero on purpose — no operating budget and a one-month launch window — and competes on clarity, proof (a narrow paid sprint before a large commitment), product rescue, ownership and transparency.
