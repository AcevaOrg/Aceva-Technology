# ACEVA Capabilities and Services

ACEVA offers five public capability and service areas: Digital Experiences, Custom Software, Mobile Products, Intelligence & Automation, and Product Rescue & Reliability. Its approved knowledge also covers artificial intelligence, machine learning, business automation, CRM and ERP systems, cloud solutions, cybersecurity consulting, API development, UI/UX, e-commerce, SaaS, maintenance and support, IT consulting, and digital transformation. The service pages define what each area includes and the limits ACEVA will not overpromise.

## Engagement approach

ACEVA may work through fixed-scope engagements, dedicated teams, hourly engagements, ongoing support arrangements, or a narrow paid sprint before a larger commitment. A fixed scope requires written scope, milestones, and acceptance criteria. A dedicated team is for continuous work. ACEVA does not accept a large build without those written controls.

## Technology guidance

The website lists current default technology guidance, including Next.js, React, TypeScript, Node.js, REST and typed APIs, PostgreSQL, LLM APIs, retrieval over client documents, workflow automation, CI, staging and rollback, secrets management, monitoring, backups, automated tests, and senior code review. Technology is selected for the project; no unverified framework, cloud host, database, provider, or certification should be promised.

## Quality and ownership

AI is used to accelerate work, not to remove responsibility. Production work is senior-reviewed, and client-owned repositories, domains, hosting, analytics, accounts, code, and data are core delivery expectations.
