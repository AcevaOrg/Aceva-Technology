# ACEVA Capabilities and Services

ACEVA offers five public capability and service areas, each described on the website as follows:

- **Digital Experiences** — websites and digital platforms designed to attract, convert and serve customers: corporate and startup websites, e-commerce and booking experiences, customer portals and dashboards, landing pages and UX/UI design systems.
- **Custom Software** — software built around the way a business actually operates: SaaS products, internal management systems, operational dashboards, marketplaces and portals, and custom web applications and integrations.
- **Mobile Products** — customer and field-team apps for iOS and Android, built with Expo and React Native by default.
- **Intelligence & Automation** — AI-assisted workflows that draw on a business's approved knowledge, connect its systems and keep a named person in charge of every critical decision.
- **Product Rescue & Reliability** — auditing unfinished, unstable, inherited or AI-generated software, separating what can be kept from what must be replaced, and setting out an evidence-led path to recovery.

Its approved knowledge also covers artificial intelligence, machine learning, business automation, CRM and ERP systems, cloud solutions, cybersecurity consulting, API development, UI/UX, e-commerce, SaaS, maintenance and support, IT consulting, and digital transformation. The service pages define what each area includes and the limits ACEVA will not overpromise.

## Engagement approach

ACEVA may work through fixed-scope engagements, dedicated teams, hourly engagements, ongoing support arrangements, or a narrow paid sprint before a larger commitment. A fixed scope requires written scope, milestones, and acceptance criteria. A dedicated team is for continuous work. ACEVA does not accept a large build without those written controls.

## Technology guidance

The website lists current default technology guidance (verified 29 October 2026), including Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Node.js, REST and typed APIs, PostgreSQL with Prisma, Expo / React Native for mobile (Flutter or native only when a requirement justifies it), LLM APIs, retrieval over client documents, workflow automation with human approval, Vercel for the frontend with AWS or Railway for backend and data, CI on every change, staging and rollback, secrets management, monitoring, backups, automated tests, a WCAG AA accessibility target, and senior code review. Technology is selected for the project; no unverified framework, cloud host, database, provider, or certification should be promised.

## Quality and ownership

AI is used to accelerate work, not to remove responsibility. Production work is senior-reviewed, and client-owned repositories, domains, hosting, analytics, accounts, code, and data are core delivery expectations.
