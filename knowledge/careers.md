# Careers at ACEVA

The current website describes a senior-led learning model: each junior has a named mentor, and no junior change reaches production without named senior review. It also emphasizes clear responsibilities, written weekly availability, and code review as scheduled teaching work.

## Current openings

The current website states that there are no open roles right now. It invites people to send work for future consideration through the general inbox.

## Application guidance

Applicants can send work and explain what they want reviewed. The published principles include no unpaid trials and no take-home exercises longer than an evening.

## HR, recruitment and job application contact

ACEVA does not publish a dedicated HR, careers or recruitment email address.
The careers page says applications are received through the general inbox,
contact@acevatech.com. That is ACEVA's general contact address, not an HR
address, so job applications and HR questions go there.
