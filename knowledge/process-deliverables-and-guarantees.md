# ACEVA Delivery Process, Handover & Warranties

ACEVA Technology adheres to an engineering delivery model defined by transparency, predictable milestones, code quality, and 100% client ownership.

---

## 1. Engagement Lifecycles & Milestones

### Step 1: Initial Qualification & Discovery
- Tell ACEVA what you are trying to build or improve.
- A senior engineering lead reviews your requirements within 24 hours.
- ACEVA responds with an honest recommendation, fit assessment, and proposed next steps.

### Step 2: Proof Sprint (Paid Prototyping)
- A focused 1 to 2-week engagement to validate requirements, design core UI wireframes, establish architecture, and prove feasibility before a major financial commitment.
- Deliverables: Interactive clickable prototype, system architecture diagram, fixed-price timeline proposal.

### Step 3: Full Custom Build & Sprints
- Agile development structured in 2-week iteration sprints.
- Weekly live working demonstrations on a staging environment (`staging.yourdomain.com`).
- Direct access to project updates, code repositories, and risk reports.

### Step 4: Quality Assurance, Security & Handover
- Automated unit testing, security vulnerability scans, accessibility audits (WCAG AA), and user acceptance testing (UAT).
- Zero-downtime deployment rollout to production cloud environment.
- Complete documentation and operational training for your internal team.

---

## 2. Deliverables & Client Guarantees

### 100% Source Code & Asset Ownership
- You own 100% of all delivered source code, designs, database schemas, and documentation.
- Repositories are transferred to your organization's GitHub/GitLab account upon completion.
- You maintain complete administrative ownership of your cloud accounts (Vercel, AWS, Stripe, domain DNS).

### Product Rescue Guarantee
- For broken or incomplete software inherited from previous teams, ACEVA performs a comprehensive audit identifying critical security vulnerabilities, auth flaws, and performance bottlenecks before any rebuild.
- Delivers a structured 30/60/90-day stabilization plan focusing first on security and stability.

### Post-Launch Support & Maintenance SLA
- 30-day post-launch warranty covering bug fixes and adjustments at zero extra cost.
- Optional ongoing maintenance SLAs covering security updates, server monitoring, backups, and feature enhancements.
