# NDAs, Confidentiality and Client IP

ACEVA's position on NDAs, confidentiality and client IP. ACEVA signs NDAs and
offers one upfront (published on /faq). The confidentiality and ownership
practices below are documented and may be described to a client. The detailed
NDA terms, data-retention periods and publicity rights are **not yet
documented**: for those, say you are not certain and point the visitor to
/contact.

## Non-disclosure agreements

ACEVA will sign an NDA before a client shares project details. The website's FAQ
answers "Will you sign an NDA before we share project details?" with: "Yes — we
offer one upfront, before any serious conversation about your product, data or
business problem." A visitor can therefore be told that an NDA is available from
the start, before any discovery conversation.

## Confidentiality practices

The approved internal source describes security-conscious practices including
controlled access, multi-factor authentication, encrypted handling of sensitive
information, confidentiality arrangements, and access revocation. These are
working practices, not public certifications, and must not be represented as
certifications or guarantees.

## Intellectual property and ownership

Client ownership is published on the website and can be stated: the client owns
the code, the accounts and the data. Domain, repository, hosting and analytics
are company-controlled during the engagement and handed to the client — never
owned personally by a developer. Clients are never locked into a proprietary
ACEVA platform. The website's terms-of-service page is still a placeholder
awaiting legal review, so the contractual wording of these terms is not yet
published.
