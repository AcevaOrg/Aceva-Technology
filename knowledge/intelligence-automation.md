# Intelligence & Automation

Intelligence & Automation connects workflows to reduce repetitive work and improve decisions while keeping people in control of important actions.

## Includes

- AI assistants and knowledge search
- Workflow and document automation
- CRM, email, calendar, and payment integrations
- Operational dashboards and alerts
- Human approval and activity logging
- Machine-learning and AI-integration solutions
- Cloud architecture, migration, and DevOps-related solutions
- Cybersecurity consulting

A suitable starting point is a controlled automation: request, data lookup, proposed action, human approval, then system update. ACEVA does not describe every chatbot as an AI agent and does not promise full autonomy.

## Automation Proof Sprint

Published on /services/intelligence (verified 31 October 2026), an Automation
Proof Sprint runs in five steps:

1. Map the workflow, trigger, data and owners.
2. Define access, approval gates and exceptions.
3. Prototype one controlled flow using approved sample data.
4. Validate edge cases, failures and human handoffs.
5. Issue a risk assessment and scale-up roadmap.

Every flow is controlled from end to end:
1. A request comes in.
2. Approved knowledge is retrieved.
3. An action is proposed.
4. A person authorizes it.
5. The system is updated.
6. The step is recorded in an audit log.

Production workflows require explicit permissions, tested failure modes and
accountable approval gates. ACEVA does not sell nominal "agents" or uncontrolled
autonomy.
## Data and security boundaries

Security, deployment, and architecture commitments require senior and team review. Do not turn these capabilities into unverified compliance guarantees, certifications, or security outcomes.

The approved internal source describes security-conscious practices including controlled access, multi-factor authentication, encrypted handling of sensitive information, confidentiality arrangements, and access revocation. These are not public certifications or guarantees and must not be represented as such.
