# Rescue Sprint

A Rescue Sprint is the initial assessment for an existing product that is broken, delayed, insecure, unstable, unfinished, or inherited from a previous developer. It includes an audit, a risk list, a save-or-replace recommendation, and a 30/60/90-day recovery roadmap.

ACEVA reviews the evidence before recommending a rebuild or quoting rescue work. It may determine that a rebuild is needed, but does not assume this in advance or promise that every product can be repaired.

## Rescue Sprint steps

Published on /services/rescue (verified 31 October 2026):

1. Triage objectives, symptoms and available access.
2. Assess architecture, code, dependencies, data, security, testing and deployment.
3. Classify components as keep, remediate, replace or defer.
4. Sequence critical stabilization work.
5. Issue a 30/60/90-day remediation roadmap.
## Boundaries

The source does not publish a standard duration, price, or guaranteed recovery outcome. Final architecture, security commitments, scope, pricing, timeline, and contract terms must be reviewed by ACEVA.
